Collaboration areas

A defined role for each team.

01 / CLINICAL & PERFUSION

Transplant and organ teams

Define an organ, donor context, comparator, perfusion conditions and function endpoints. Evaluate kidney, liver, heart and lung on their own terms.

02 / METABOLISM & PRODUCT

Measurement and formulation

Characterize oxygen affinity, temperature response, oxidation, stability and tissue-level readouts before testing any clinical promise.

03 / SPECIALIST PATHWAYS

Xenografts and organ engineering

Study possible compatibility with gene-edited donors, ex-vivo interventions or engineered grafts in separate studies. BHOC itself is not a genetic editing method.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

Manufacturing and regulation

Plan quality systems, safety work, manufacturing capacity and regulatory discussions appropriate to the product and setting.
