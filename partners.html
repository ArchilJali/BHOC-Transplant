01 / CLINICAL & PERFUSION

Transplant and organ teams

02 / METABOLISM & PRODUCT

Characterize oxygen affinity, temperature response, oxidation, stability and tissue-level readouts before testing any clinical promise.

Nature is the engineer.
We are still learning.

“All life needs oxygen”* expresses why we focus on oxygen-dependent human cells and transplanted organs. Nature has shaped hemoglobin-based oxygen binding over hundreds of millions of years.

We learn from its reversible binding. For BHOC, an “unlimited source of material”** means starting material independent of human blood donation and available through scalable supply chains worldwide. For a defined BHOC product, oxygen release as local pressure changes, behavior during cooling and rewarming, and any role of nitric oxide in vascular-flow regulation*** must be measured. Independent evidence should correct both our understanding and the technology.

Jeff Parke

Founder and Executive Director, Cold Ischemia Foundation. In his own account, he describes roughly two decades on dialysis and three kidney transplants. He also brings experience in clinical research.

Context and evolving understanding

* “All life needs oxygen” is shorthand, not a literal biological rule. Anaerobic organisms can live without molecular oxygen; some obtain energy using other electron acceptors or pathways. Here the focus is oxygen-dependent human tissue.

** “Unlimited” describes independence from the human blood donor pool and broad availability of starting material, not a literally infinite supply. The BHOC manufacturing architecture is based on single-use technology (SUT), modular robotic production and online QC/QA oversight, designed for scalable deployment in different regions. Product output, consistency and quality systems must be demonstrated for each production site.

*** Cell-free hemoglobin can consume nitric oxide and alter vascular tone. Whether a defined BHOC formulation has a useful and safe vascular effect remains to be tested. Read the BHOC NO evidence ↗
