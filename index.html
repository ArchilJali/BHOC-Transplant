Before · During · After transplantation

Oxygen continuity. Across every transition.

BHOC Transplant explores how BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier), a cell-free hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier, could support oxygen delivery during organ preservation, machine perfusion and transplantation. Its research follows oxygen continuity from donor care through ex vivo organ support to recipient recovery. Each stage requires its own evidence and protocol.
